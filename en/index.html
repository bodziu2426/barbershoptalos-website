About us

We are a team of experienced barbers working in the centre of Wrocław, at ul. Poniatowskiego 7e and Cybulskiego 3. We focus on quality, precision and an individual approach to every client. Over 1400 reviews on Booksy with a 5.0 rating put us among the best-rated barbers in Wrocław. Our barbershop is a place where you can relax and leave with a haircut that highlights your style.
